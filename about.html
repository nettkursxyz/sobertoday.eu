About Sober Today

Sober Today is a free, accessible self-help tool for reflection, notes and keeping track of sober days. You do not need an account, and there is no tracking. Your data is stored locally on your device.

Who is Sober Today for?

Sober Today is for anyone who wants to change their relationship with alcohol. Perhaps you want a break, perhaps you want to be sober today, or perhaps you want to better understand which situations, thoughts and choices make it easier to avoid drinking.

You do not have to make a decision for the rest of your life. The idea is simpler: to give you a place to follow your own progress, write down what matters to you and learn from what works.

Sober Today is a self-help tool and does not replace medical treatment or professional support. If you need more support, you can use it alongside help from your doctor, a healthcare professional, a counsellor, an organisation or someone you trust.

A place for your thoughts

Sober Today counts your sober days. You can start today, choose an earlier date or set a future date if you want to plan when to begin. Along the way, you can write notes and reflect on why you want alcohol-free days, how you feel and what you want to remember from your journey.

You will find a motivation page with practical tips and a social survival guide.

A setback does not undo your journey

Imagine taking a long walk and making a detour along the way. The detour does not mean the whole walk was wasted. You still walked all the miles that came before.

The same applies here. If you drink again after a period of sober days, you do not have to erase what you have already done or learned. In Settings, you can reset your sober day counter while keeping your history and notes.

You are not starting from scratch. You are continuing with the experience you have already gained.

Private and without tracking

The idea behind the project is simple: a self-help tool should support you without monitoring you. You do not need an account or have to tell us who you are.

  • No registration: You do not need to create an account.
  • No tracking: We do not monitor your activity or build a profile of you.
  • Local storage: Your journal entries are stored locally on your own phone or computer using localStorage. You own your data and can delete it with one click.
  • You decide: You can export, import or delete your data yourself.

Because your data is stored locally in your browser, use the same browser and device to see the same history. You can also export a backup from Settings.

How is your data stored?

Sober Today uses localStorage, which is part of your browser’s Web Storage technology. It allows the website to store information directly in the browser on your device. Data in localStorage usually remains when you close and reopen your browser, and is not automatically sent to the server.

Storage is tied to your browser and the sobertoday.eu website. Your notes do not automatically follow you if you switch browsers or devices. Clearing your browser data may also remove data in localStorage. We therefore recommend exporting a backup regularly from Settings.

Reminders without email or push notifications

If you would like a check-in reminder, you can download a simple calendar file (.ics). The reminder lives in your own calendar. Sober Today does not need your email address or send you push notifications.

  • You choose the name: Rename the calendar event to something that suits you.
  • You choose the frequency: Daily, weekly or whenever works for you.
  • You stay in control: Your calendar manages the reminder, rather than Sober Today.
Important: Stopping alcohol suddenly can sometimes be dangerous. If you are unsure whether you are physically dependent or are experiencing severe withdrawal symptoms, contact your doctor, an urgent care service or another healthcare professional for guidance and safe support.

About the project
